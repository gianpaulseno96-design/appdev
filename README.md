# 🎬Lilo & Stitch
  <img width="1200" height="1800" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4abbbec4-7957-4e32-9a04-b89ca78ce281" />
  
*This is my family. I found it, all on my own. It's little, and broken, but still good. Yeah. Still good." - Stitch pleading to stay with Lilo and Nani.*

*Lilo & Stitch is a lonely Hawaiian girl named Lilo who adopts a destructive alien fugitive named Stitch, helping mend her grieving family through the concept of ʻohana (family).*

# ❤️Why I love It 
*  *The Nostalgia:* 2000s Childhood Staple: Released in 2002 alongside a long-running Disney Channel series, direct-to-video sequels, and video games, Lilo & Stitch became a core childhood memory for Millennials and Gen Z ("Zillennials")
*  *The Characters:* Lilo Pelekai, Stitch (Experiment 626), Nani Pelekai, David Kawena and Dr. Jumba Jookiba & Pleakley
*   *The Upside Down:* Iconic Visual & Merch Pose: Because Stitch is chaotic and gravity-defying—often climbing walls, hanging from ceilings, or doing headstands—"Upside Down Stitch" is a signature pose used across Loungefly backpacks, vinyl figures, stickers, and GIFs.

  # 🌸 Quick facts

| Features | Details |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Novel Adaption |  The 2002 movie was written from an original pitch by co-director Chris Sanders rather than adapted from an existing novel. |
| Core Genres |  Lilo & Stitch blends science fiction, comedy-drama, and family adventure. |
| Release | Lilo & Stitch June 21, 2002 (US) |
| Season |  Lilo & Stitch: The Series ran for 2 seasons totaling 65 episodes (39 in Season 1 and 26 in Season 2) |

*"We were given a miracle because of you. We are a family because of you. And nothing could ever be broken that we can't fix together."*  - Abuela Alma

*Encanto is  is a 2021 Disney animated musical fantasy film about the Madrigals, an extraordinary Colombian family living in a sentient magical house where every child receives a unique superpower—except Mirabel, who must uncover why their magic is fading.*

# ❤️Why I love It 
*  *The Nostalgia:* Intergenerational Family Memories and Classic Disney Musical Revival
*  *The Characters:* Mirabel Madrigal, Abuela Alma Madrigal, Isabela & Luisa Madrigal, Bruno Madrigal and Julieta, Pepa, Dolores, Camilo & Antonio
*   *The Upside Down:* Turning the Family Dynamic Upside Down, Fan "Reversed Roles" AUs and Hidden Visual Easter Egg

  # 🌸 Quick facts

| Features | Details |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Novel Adaption | Adaptation	Original story (not based on an existing novel). Disney released tie-in books, including Disney Encanto: The Junior Novelization by Angela Cervantes (2021), a full-color graphic novel, and spin-off books like A Tale of Three Sisters and Nightmares and Sueños (2024). |
| Core Genres | Animated musical, fantasy, comedy-drama, and family adventure. |
| Release |	November 24, 2021 (US theatrical release) and December 24, 2021 (streaming on Disney+). |
| Season | Standalone feature film (no official TV seasons) |


# 🎬Barbie as the Island Princess
<img width="265" height="376" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/84354828-e2fc-45a5-b72c-bc211e3e9765" />

*"Joy's not found in things such as necklaces and rings, but in love that's always there: that's the jewel beyond compare."*  - Princess Luciana

*Barbie as the Island Princess is a 2007 CGI animated musical film—the eleventh entry in the Barbie movie franchise—about Rosella ("Ro"), a shipwrecked girl raised by talking animals on a tropical island who travels to a royal kingdom and uncovers both her true heritage and a treacherous plot to steal the throne.*

# ❤️Why I love It 
*  *The Nostalgia:* 2000s Barbie Golden Era and Memorable Soundtrack
*  *The Characters:* Rosella ("Ro"), Prince Antonio, Sagi, Azul & Tika, Queen Ariana, Princess Luciana and King Peter, Queen Danielle & Queen Marissa
*   *The Upside Down:* Turning Ro's World Upside Down. As noted in the film's official IMDb synopsis, Ro sees her peaceful island world "turn upside down" when Prince Antonio takes her and her animal friends into royal civilization—forcing a girl who climbs trees and talks to animals to adapt to ballgowns, tea parties, and palace intrigue.

  
  # 🌸 Quick facts

| Features | Details |
| ----------- | ----------- |

| Release |	September 18, 2007 (US direct-to-DVD release) and September 23, 2007 (Nickelodeon TV premiere). |
